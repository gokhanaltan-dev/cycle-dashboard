          Method

          Macro phase

          Eight growth series (Chicago Fed activity index, jobless claims, building permits, industrial production, consumer sentiment, Sahm rule, Philly Fed and Empire State surveys) are converted to monthly, transformed (level, YoY or 3-month average), z-scored against their own rolling 10-year history and sign-adjusted so higher = stronger. The composite is their equal-weight average; series released late are carried forward up to two months. Level = 3-month average of the composite. Momentum = 3-month change in level, scaled by its own 10-year volatility. Quadrant gives the phase; a small dead-band stops flip-flopping near the axes. Confidence blends distance from the axes with how many indicators agree.

          Market-implied phase

          Each session the 11 sectors are ranked on blended 3- and 6-month performance relative to SPY. That ranking is correlated (Spearman) with the classic leadership template for each phase (Early: financials, discretionary, real estate, industrials, tech, materials; Mid: tech, communications; Late: energy, materials, staples, health care, utilities; Recession: staples, utilities, health care). Scores are smoothed with a 10-day EMA and a new phase must lead for 5 straight sessions before the call changes.

          Relative Rotation Graph

          An approximation of the JdK method (the original formula is proprietary). RS = ETF ÷ SPY, lightly smoothed. RS-Ratio = 100 + z-score of RS against its rolling mean (14 weeks weekly, 63 sessions daily). RS-Momentum = 100 + rate of change of RS-Ratio (4 weeks / 10 sessions) divided by its own volatility. Quadrant labels use a ±0.25 dead-band so ETFs hugging an axis don't flicker.

          Exposure verdict

          Counts passed checks (index trend, risk-appetite ratios, credit spreads, VIX) and subtracts one if the macro phase is Recession. All but one passed = full exposure; down to three missed = normal; at least one net = reduced; otherwise defensive.

          Caveats

          • The rotation model is a historical tendency, not a law. Phases vary in length and sometimes get skipped.
          • The backtest uses today's revised macro data, so it flatters real-time accuracy. The call log above is the honest record going forward.
          • Futures and index series (copper, gold, VIX, dollar) trade on different calendars and are aligned to US equity sessions.
